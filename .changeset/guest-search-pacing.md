Pace guest search so it can't exhaust the Spotify quota, and keep Spotify cooldowns across restarts.

- Search now runs against a per-provider search budget (`SEARCH_BUDGET_LIMIT` per `SEARCH_BUDGET_WINDOW_MS`, default 3 per 30s) plus a per-guest limit (`SEARCH_GUEST_LIMIT`) and a shared anonymous limit (`SEARCH_ANON_LIMIT`) for tokenless callers. Cached and in-flight-shared searches are always served and never count.
- New refusals: 429 `search_too_fast` and 503 `search_busy`, each with `retryAfterSec` and a `Retry-After` header. An open provider cooldown still returns 503 `provider_rate_limited` without spending budget.
- A client that retries before the wait it was given is refused and charged against its own limit, so clients ignoring `Retry-After` stop winning freed search slots from guests who wait.
- Daily search cap: Spotify searches are also capped per provider at `SEARCH_DAILY_LIMIT` per rolling 24h (default 400, under development mode's ~500/day quota), spent together with the 30s budget. Once spent, search answers 503 `search_daily_limit` with `retryAfterSec` and `Retry-After`.
- Local catalog fallback: tracks from provider search results and accepted queue requests are kept in a new `track_catalog` table (migration 0010; backfilled once from `queue_items`). When the budget is busy, the daily cap is spent, or the provider is rate limiting search, matching known tracks are returned as a 200 with `source: 'local'` and `fallbackReason`, spending nothing; provider answers carry `source: 'provider'`. With no local match the usual refusal is returned, and `search_too_fast` and early-retry refusals never get local results.
- Successful searches carry `slowDown: { debounceMs, ttlSec }` when the budget is nearly spent (`SEARCH_SLOWDOWN_*`).
- Search accepts optional guest auth (`Authorization: Bearer <slotToken>`); `QueueApi.search` takes `{ slotToken, signal }`.
- Provider cooldowns are kept per quota group (`search` for `/v1/search`, `player` for everything else), so a search quota no longer pauses now-playing or queue pushes, and only player cooldowns are announced to the party. They persist in `provider_connections.rate_limited_until` / `search_rate_limited_until` (migrations 0008, 0009), clamped to `PROVIDER_COOLDOWN_MAX_SEC` (default 24h), and `StreamingRouter` seeds them back on load so a restart never calls the provider inside a cooldown. Each stored cooldown records the Spotify client id (`rate_limited_client_id`); one from a different or unknown client id is ignored.
- Provider 429s log their `reason` and `Retry-After`, and per-endpoint provider call counts log every 30s.
- Optional search sources: `SEARCH_SOURCES` (default `provider,catalog`) sets where guest search answers from, in order; `deezer` adds Deezer's public search (`source: 'external'`, `opendj:isrc:<ISRC>` uris, own `DEEZER_RATE_LIMIT` per 5s, no provider quota spent; asks Deezer with `order=RANKING`), and such uris are resolved to the host's provider when requested (catalog by ISRC, then Sonovault with `SONOVAULT_API_KEY`, then a quota-checked provider ISRC search), else 422 `track_unresolvable`. Non-catalog resolutions for Spotify are verified against Spotify's public oEmbed title (karaoke/cover/tribute/instrumental markers or a different title reject the answer and the next resolver is asked; an unavailable check accepts); `TRACK_VERIFY=off` disables it. `track_catalog` gains an `isrc` column (migration 0011); `Track` gains optional `isrc`.
- `sonovault` search source (`SEARCH_SOURCES`, needs `SONOVAULT_API_KEY`; listed without a key it is disabled with a startup warning): queries shaped `Artist - Title` (also `–`/`—`, retried swapped when empty) or `Title by Artist` are sent to Sonovault's artist + title search (at most two calls; other queries are skipped without a call) and answer `source: 'external'` `opendj:isrc:<ISRC>` tracks without album art. It shares Sonovault's 18/min budget with add-time resolution and leaves 6 calls of it for resolution.
- Low-confidence Deezer escalation: when Deezer's answer has no popular original (no non-variant hit matching every query word with a Deezer rank of at least `DEEZER_CONFIDENT_RANK`, default 500000; queries under 3 characters never count) and `SEARCH_SOURCES` lists `provider` after `deezer`, the provider is asked through the usual budget, daily cap, and cooldown admission without charging the guest a second unit. A provider answer is returned as `source: 'provider'`; if the provider can't be asked or fails, the Deezer answer is returned as `source: 'external'`. Each escalation logs `[search] deezer low-confidence q=<q> → provider served|refused:<reason>`.
